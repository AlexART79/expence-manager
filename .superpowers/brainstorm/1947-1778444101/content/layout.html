How should the app be laid out?

Navigation placement shapes everything — choose what fits your workflow best.

A — Sidebar Nav

Vertical icon+label nav on the left. More screen real estate for content. Standard for dashboards (VS Code, Figma, Notion). Collapses to icons on mobile.

B — Top Nav

Horizontal nav bar at the top. Familiar, web-native. Works well with few nav items (3-5). The current app already uses this — a refined version.

C — Bottom Tab Bar

Mobile-first tab bar at the bottom. Best for mobile/PWA use. Thumb-friendly, simple. Less suited to complex desktop dashboards.
